About Savanna

Savanna breaks free from the conventional slide decks, placing a strong emphasis on web publishing. Transform your slides into dynamic web apps, interactive audience-driven presentations, engaging games, captivating websites, and much more. With Savanna, you're not just presenting—you're redefining how content is shared and experienced.

Redefine Presentations

  • Responsive Design: Not just for desktop presentations – adjust it beautifully for mobile portrait mode as well!
  • Interactive Experiences: Support programmable scripts and interactive elements to boost audience participation.
  • Immersive Animations: Leverage the best features of traditional slides—such as animations and morph transitions—for the web.

Simplify Creation

  • Seamless PowerPoint Compatibility: Reuse your existing assets—templates, branding, fonts, and more—without learning new tools.
  • Intuitive Drag-and-Drop Interface: Extensive application of drag and drop to make creating stunning content fast and effortless.
  • Generative AI Assistance: Tap into generative AI for innovative design suggestions and a creative edge with minimal effort.

Savanna empowers you to go beyond slides, delivering impactful, interactive, and immersive experiences with ease.
